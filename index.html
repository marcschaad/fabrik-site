About

Started in Brooklyn. Now in Orange County.

Fabrik Collective began in Brooklyn on one idea: bring in the people the work needs, and only for as long as it needs them. That idea carried us through brand and strategy work for growing companies and for L'Oréal. Today Fabrik is run from Irvine by its founders, Chira and Marc Schaad, with trusted specialists on call. Same idea, more experience behind it.
